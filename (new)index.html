About the Symposium

Where Chemistry
Finds Its Symphony

ChemSymphoria is the annual in-house chemistry symposium of IISER Pune — a celebration of scientific discovery orchestrated by the Department of Chemistry and the Chemphilic student club.

The event brings together distinguished researchers, faculty, and students for talks, poster sessions, competitions, and vibrant discourse at the frontier of chemical science.

Distinguished Invited Talks
Lectures from leading chemists at the frontier of research across India and abroad.
Research Poster Sessions
Students and scholars present cutting-edge research in an open, engaging format.
Scientific Competitions
Chemistry quizzes, lab challenges, and science communication events for all participants.
