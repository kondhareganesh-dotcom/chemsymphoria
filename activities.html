Events & Competitions

Activities & Competitions

ChemSymphoria goes beyond lectures — it's a festival of chemistry offering competitions, creative events, and collaborative experiences. From rigorous quiz rounds to artistic explorations of molecular beauty, there's something for every chemist.

Join the Fun

Six Events Across Two Days

All registered participants are welcome to take part in the competitions and activities. Pre-registration for competitive events is recommended and sign-ups open at the event registration desk.

Chemistry Quiz

Test your chemical knowledge

Competitive

A multi-round quiz spanning the entire breadth of chemical science — organic, inorganic, physical, analytical, and biochemistry. Rounds include multiple choice, rapid-fire, visual identification, and a challenging final round. Open to all registered participants regardless of level.

Duration: 2 hours
Team of 2–3 participants
Multiple rounds: Prelims → Semi → Grand Final
Day 1, 16:00 · C.V. Raman Auditorium Register Interest

Poster Presentation

Present your research

Research

Present your research to a broad chemistry audience through a scientific poster. PhD scholars and project students are encouraged to showcase their work in a welcoming and intellectually stimulating environment. Faculty judges will evaluate posters on scientific content, clarity, and the presenter's ability to communicate their work.

Afternoon session · Both days
Individual presenter
A0 poster format (portrait orientation)
Days 1 & 2 · Poster Gallery Submit Abstract

Science Communication

Make chemistry accessible

Creative

Can you explain a complex chemistry concept to someone with no background in the field? That's the challenge. Using any medium — models, analogies, visuals, storytelling, or demonstrations — participants have 5 minutes to communicate the wonder of chemistry to a general audience. All academic backgrounds welcome.

5-minute presentations
Individual or team (up to 2)
Judged by faculty panel
Day 2, 15:30 · Seminar Hall Register Interest

ChemArt

Chemistry through creative expression

Creative

Where science meets art. Participants create artwork inspired by chemistry — from molecular structures and crystal lattices to lab aesthetics, elemental beauty, and reaction dynamics. Any medium is accepted: sketching, digital art, photography, painting, sculpture, or mixed media. Express your unique vision of the molecular world.

1-hour creative session
Individual participation
Multiple media accepted · Displayed at venue
Day 1 · Art Exhibition Space Register Interest

Lab Techniques Challenge

Experimental skills under pressure

Practical

Demonstrate your experimental prowess in a timed practical setting. Participants carry out specific laboratory techniques and are assessed on accuracy, technique, safety, and speed. Designed for BS/MS/PhD students who want to put their bench skills to the test in a friendly and challenging competition environment.

90 minutes
Individual · BS/MS/PhD participants
Safety gear required · Provided on-site
Day 1 · Teaching Laboratories Register Interest

Open Mic & Cultural Night

Science, art, and community

Cultural

An evening celebrating the beautiful overlap between science and human creativity. The Open Mic & Cultural Night features music, poetry, spoken word, stand-up, and performances by students and participants. No theme restriction — bring your talent. The only rule: chemistry should spark some joy.

Evening, Day 1 (17:30 onwards)
Open to all participants
Sign up at the registration desk
Day 1, 17:30 · Open Amphitheatre Sign Up
