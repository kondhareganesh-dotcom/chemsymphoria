Two Days of Science

Program Schedule

The detailed schedule is being finalised. The outline below reflects the planned structure for ChemSymphoria 2026–27 — a two-day celebration of chemistry with plenary lectures, invited talks, poster sessions, competitions, and cultural events.

Draft Programme: Times and sessions are indicative and subject to change. The finalised schedule with speaker names and talk titles will be published before the event.
