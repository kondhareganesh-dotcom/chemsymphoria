About the Event

A Celebration of
Chemical Science

ChemSymphoria is the annual in-house chemistry symposium of IISER Pune, proudly organized by the Department of Chemistry and the Chemphilic student club. Each edition gathers students, researchers, and faculty to celebrate Chemistry Day with scientific talks, creative competitions, and an enduring spirit of discovery.

Our Foundations

What Drives ChemSymphoria

ChemSymphoria stands on four core principles that shape every edition of the symposium — from the quality of scientific discourse to the warmth of community built between participants.

01

Scientific Excellence

We bring together cutting-edge research talks, plenary lectures, and poster sessions that span the breadth of chemical science — from synthesis and spectroscopy to computational chemistry and materials science. Every session is curated to inspire and challenge participants at every level.

02

Community & Collaboration

ChemSymphoria is as much about the conversations between talks as the talks themselves. We foster an environment where students freely engage with faculty, researchers share ideas across disciplines, and friendships between chemists are forged over shared curiosity and a common passion for the molecular world.

03

Student Leadership

Organised entirely by the Chemphilic student club of IISER Pune's Department of Chemistry, ChemSymphoria is a testament to student initiative. From logistics and design to speaker outreach and content curation, every aspect of the symposium is driven by the passion and professionalism of student organizers.

04

Institutional Heritage

Rooted in the rich academic traditions of IISER Pune — one of India's premier science research institutes — ChemSymphoria carries forward a legacy of intellectual rigour, interdisciplinary thinking, and a deep respect for the transformative power of basic science and fundamental chemistry research.

Our Institution

IISER Pune — Science at its Finest

The Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune (IISER Pune) was established in 2006 under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It is one of eight premier institutions dedicated to fostering excellence in science education and fundamental research across the country.

The Department of Chemistry at IISER Pune is renowned for its world-class research spanning organic, inorganic, physical, and theoretical chemistry. With a distinguished faculty body, well-equipped research facilities, and a vibrant doctoral programme, the department has produced significant contributions to global chemistry research.

ChemSymphoria emerges from this culture of excellence — a student-led celebration that honours the tradition of scientific inquiry that IISER Pune has championed since its inception, while also celebrating the joy and wonder that chemistry brings to all who study it.

C.V. Raman Auditorium

ChemSymphoria 2026–27 is hosted at the C.V. Raman Auditorium, the flagship venue of IISER Pune, named in honour of Nobel Laureate Sir Chandrasekhara Venkata Raman. The auditorium provides a world-class setting for plenary lectures, cultural events, and the valedictory ceremony.
