Welcome to CS: 2026-27

ChemSymphoria at IISER Pune represents an annual impactful chemical research and academic collaboration. As our flagship inhouse symposium for 2026-27, we unite ground breaking molecular insights with sophisticated research to define the future of the chemical sciences. IISER Pune Chemistry department fosters scientific curiosity, intellectual exchange and research excellence in the form of a faculty-student forum organized by the Chemphilic Team. ChemSymphoria, the annual in-house Chemistry symposium, is a culmination of the vibrant spirit of our Chemistry community at IISER Pune. ChemSymphoria is ignited by our enthusiasm for chemistry. It is aimed at fostering a departmental spirit, inspiring new ideas and building a sense of connection within us all.
